Why you want this

👀 Front-end PRs you can SEE

A diff says what changed in the code; a before/after says what changed for the user. When every UI pull request ships with its pixels, review gets faster and "LGTM" means someone actually looked.

🪣 A photo album, by accident

Content-hashed keys, dated paths: every shot stays in your bucket forever. Six months in, you own what no git history gives you — what your app looked like, release by release. Retrospectives write themselves.

How it works

📸

1. Capture

Bun.WebView renders the page — system WKWebView on macOS, your Chrome elsewhere. Nothing to install, retina PNGs out.

cliche localhost:3000 shot.png
→
🪣

2. Upload

Bun.S3Client pushes it to your bucket — R2, S3, MinIO — under a content-hashed, dated key that never breaks.

--upload --prefix pr-123
→
🔗

3. Paste

The public URL lands on stdout (--markdown for a ready-made image line). Straight into the PR body.

gh pr edit 123 --body "…"

What's in the box

  • Capture — Bun.WebView: system WKWebView on macOS, your installed Chrome on Linux/Windows. Viewports, --full-page for the whole page in one shot, --wait-for, --scroll-to, --local-storage to boot authenticated screens.
  • Upload — Bun.S3Client: R2, AWS S3, MinIO, anything S3-compatible, configured by the standard S3_* env vars you already have.
  • URL out — bare URLs on stdout by default, --markdown for ready-to-paste ![caption](url) lines.
  • MCP in — cliche mcp serves screenshot and upload to any MCP client over stdio. Hand-rolled JSON-RPC, still zero dependencies.
  • One-command R2 setup — cliche setup creates the bucket and its public URL through your wrangler login, and writes the config to .env.
  • Claude Code skill — bunx @voila.dev/cliche skill installs the PR-screenshots skill into .claude/skills/. Also served at /skill.md.
  • The album — bunx @voila.dev/cliche album opens the bucket as a browsable photo album of your app, month by month.
